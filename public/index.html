What a website can learn about you

When you visit a random site, it cannot read your LinkedIn cookies or your Gmail sessions — modern browsers block cross-site cookies. But it can still identify you without cookies, using the signals shown below. This page runs those same checks on your own browser and shows you exactly what's exposed. Nothing is sent anywhere until you click "Save this visit".
